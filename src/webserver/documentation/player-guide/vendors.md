Some characters in the world are vendors. They sell items for coins, and they buy what you no longer need.

## Finding a vendor

Vendors are marked on your minimap with a pale pin that shows a gold coin. The pin's point stands on the door of the house the vendor sells in, so walk to it and go inside. A vendor who stands out in the open is marked where they stand.

## Opening a vendor

Walk up to the character and talk to them, the same way you talk to a quest giver. If they have something to sell, the vendor window opens and your bags open with it.

If the character also has quests for you, you see their conversation first. Choose **Browse goods** to open the vendor window.

The window closes when you walk away, press **Escape** or select **Close**.

## Buying

The **Buy** tab lists what the vendor sells, each with its price.

1. Set how many you want in the box beside the item. It starts at 1 and goes up to 999.
2. Select **Buy**.

| What you see | What it means |
|--------------|---------------|
| A price in red, and a greyed out Buy button | You cannot afford that many |
| "Free" | The vendor gives the item away |
| "Your bags are full." | An item you do not hold yet needs a free bag slot. More of something you already hold does not. |

A vendor never runs out of stock.

## Selling

With the vendor window open, **right-click** an item in your bags. On a phone or tablet, **double-tap** it instead. The whole stack is sold at once for its sell price.

Hover over any item to see its sell price at the bottom of the tooltip, on the line that starts with "Sells for".

Some things cannot be sold:

- Quest items.
- Items a vendor has no use for. Their tooltip shows no "Sells for" line.
- The item you are wearing, and a bag that is in one of your bag slots. If you hold more of the same item, the extra ones are sold and the one in use stays.

:::tip Every vendor pays the same
What you get for an item does not depend on the vendor. Any vendor buys any item at that item's sell price.
:::

## Buying back

Sold something by mistake? Open the **Buyback** tab. It lists the last 8 things you sold, newest first. Select **Buy back** to get the items for exactly what you were paid.

The list is cleared when you log out, so buy back before you leave.

## Good to know

- You need to be alive and standing next to the vendor for every purchase and sale.
- You cannot use a vendor while a trade with another player is open.
- A sale is refused if the coins would not fit in your purse.

See [Inventory and Equipment](#/player-guide/inventory-and-equipment) for your bags and coins, and [Trading](#/player-guide/trading) for swapping items with other players.
