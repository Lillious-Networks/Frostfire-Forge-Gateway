Everything you own lives in your inventory. Press **B** to open it. Your coins are shown at the bottom of the same window.

## The inventory

- You start with **25** item slots.
- Items that stack show a number for how many you hold.
- Point at an item to read its details.
- Item names are coloured by quality.

| Quality | Colour |
|---------|--------|
| Common | Grey |
| Uncommon | Green |
| Rare | Blue |
| Epic | Purple |
| Legendary | Orange |

### Organising items

Drag an item onto another slot to move it. If the slot is taken, the two items swap places. On a touch screen, hold the item first and then drag it. Your layout is saved with your character.

### Bags

The inventory window has four bag slots. Each bag you equip adds more item slots.

| What you want | How to do it |
|---------------|--------------|
| Equip a bag | Double-click the bag, or drag it onto a bag slot. On a phone or tablet, double-tap it. |
| Take a bag off | Double-click it in its bag slot. On a phone or tablet, double-tap it. |

You cannot take a bag off while items sit in the slots it adds. Move those items first.

### Destroying items

To get rid of an item for good, drag it out of the inventory and drop it on the game world. A window asks you to confirm. For **Epic** and **Legendary** items you have to type `delete` before the button works.

:::warning
A destroyed item is gone. It cannot be brought back.
:::

## Using items

Double-click an item to use it. On a touch screen, double-tap it.

| Item | What a double-click does |
|------|--------------------------|
| Equipment and bags | Equips it |
| Consumables, such as potions and food | Uses one. See [Consumables](#/player-guide/consumables). |
| Your home item | Starts the cast that takes you home. See [Inns and Your Home](#/player-guide/inns). |

## Equipment

Press **C** to open the character window. It shows your character, your stats and your equipment slots.

| Area | Slots |
|------|-------|
| Armour | Helmet, Shoulderguards, Chestplate, Wristguards, Gloves, Belt, Pants, Boots |
| Jewellery | Necklace, two Rings, two Trinkets |
| Weapons | Weapon, Off-hand Weapon |

| What you want | How to do it |
|---------------|--------------|
| Equip an item | Double-click it in your inventory. The item you were wearing goes back into your inventory. |
| Take an item off | Double-click it in the character window, or drag it into your inventory |
| Compare with what you wear | Hold **Shift** while pointing at an equipment item |
| Look at another player's gear | Right-click the player and choose **Inspect** |

Things to know:

- Some items need a certain level. The item's details show **Requires Level** and the number. Below that level, the item will not equip.
- You cannot change equipment while in combat. You see "You can't change equipment while in combat." Wait a few seconds after the fight.
- Equipment changes your stats: Health, Mana, Damage, Armor, Critical Chance, Critical Damage and Avoidance.

## Currency

There are three coins: **gold**, **silver** and **copper**.

| Coin | Worth |
|------|-------|
| 1 silver | 100 copper |
| 1 gold | 100 silver |

Coins convert upwards automatically, so you never carry more than 99 copper or 99 silver. You earn coins from defeated creatures, from quest rewards and by selling items to [vendors](#/player-guide/vendors).

## Loot from creatures

A creature you defeated may leave loot on its body.

1. Stand close to the body.
2. **Right-click** it. On a touch screen, tap it.
3. The **Loot** window lists the items and coins.
4. Select **Take All**, or untick what you do not want and select **Take Selected**.

Messages you may see:

| Message | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| "You are too far away to loot that." | Move closer to the body |
| "You don't have permission to loot that corpse." | The loot belongs to someone else |
| "There is nothing to loot." | The body is empty |

In a party, the right to loot the items passes from member to member with each kill, and the coins are split evenly. See [Parties](#/player-guide/parties).

## Loot on the ground

Sometimes an item appears floating above the ground with a glow in its quality colour.

- Some ground items have an owner. If one is not yours, you see **Owned by** and a name when you get near, and you cannot take it.
- A ground item with no owner is free for anyone. The first player to pick it up gets it.
- Stand next to an item you can take and tap **E** to pick up the nearest one.
- Hold **E** to pick up everything you can take that is close by.
- Point at a ground item to read what it is.

:::note
Ground loot does not wait forever. It disappears after 30 minutes. An item with an owner also disappears about 5 minutes after that owner leaves the game.
:::

## Loot chests

When you find a loot chest, stand next to it and press **F**. The **Loot Chest** window opens with **Take Selected** and **Take All**, just like creature loot.

A chest gives every player their own contents, so nobody can empty it before you. Once you have taken everything, the chest tells you "This chest is empty for you."

## Running out of space

Keep a few slots free. When quest rewards do not fit in your bags, the turn-in is refused with "You don't have enough bag space." until you make room. Equip more bags or destroy what you do not need.
