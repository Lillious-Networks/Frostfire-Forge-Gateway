A trade lets two players swap items and coins. Each of you puts what you offer on your own side of a window, and nothing changes hands until you have both accepted.

:::note
Trading needs a full account. Guests cannot trade.
:::

## Starting a trade

1. Stand next to the player you want to trade with.
2. **Right-click** them and choose **Trade**. On a touch screen, hold your finger on the player to open the menu.
3. The player sees a **Trade Request** window with your name. You get the message "Trade request sent to" followed by their name.
4. When they select **Accept**, the trade window opens for both of you, and your bags open with it.

You can be in one trade at a time.

## The trade window

The window has two sides.

| Side | What it shows |
|------|---------------|
| You offer | The items and coins you are putting up. You change this side. |
| The other player's name | What they are putting up. You can only look. |

Each side holds up to **8** different items, plus gold, silver and copper.

### Offering items

There are two ways to put an item on your side:

- **Drag** it from your bags onto your side of the window.
- **Right-click** it in your bags.

On a phone or tablet, **double-tap** the item in your bags instead. The trade window sits at the left of the screen there, so your bags stay open beside it.

One of the item goes in. To offer more of a stack, raise the number on the item.

To take an item back, select the **×** on it (it shows when you hover over the item, and always on a touch screen), or right-click it.

### Offering coins

Type the gold, silver and copper you want to give into the three fields under your items. You cannot offer more than you have.

### What cannot be traded

- Quest items.
- The item you are wearing, and a bag that is in one of your bag slots. If you hold more of the same item, the extra ones can be traded.

You cannot put on equipment or bags while a trade is open.

Hover over an item on the other side to read what it is before you accept.

## Accepting

When you are happy with both sides, select **Accept**. The trade is made as soon as you have both accepted.

| What you see | What it means |
|--------------|---------------|
| A side turns green and says **Accepted** | That player has accepted the trade as it stands |
| "The offer changed. Look it over." | Someone changed their side. Accept is off for 2 seconds so you can check. |
| "Waiting for" the other player | You have accepted and they have not yet |
| "Trade complete." | Everything changed hands |

:::tip Check before you accept
Whenever either side changes, both accepts are taken back. Nobody can swap an item at the last moment and keep your accept. Read the other side again each time it changes.
:::

A trade is all or nothing. If one of you no longer has something you offered, or the coins would not fit in the other player's purse, nothing moves and you are told why.

## When a trade is cancelled

Select **Cancel** or press **Escape** to stop a trade. Nothing changes hands.

A trade is also cancelled when either of you:

- walks away, or goes to another map
- enters combat
- dies
- logs out

## Staying safe

- Only what is in the window is part of the trade. A promise made in chat is not.
- Read both sides before every accept.
- If a player tricks you, report them: right-click the player and choose **Report Player**. The admins can see every completed trade.
