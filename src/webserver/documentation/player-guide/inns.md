---
title: Inns and Your Home
description: Set your home at an innkeeper and use your home item to return there from anywhere.
order: 63
---

Every character carries a home item. Using it takes you back to your home, from anywhere in the world. You choose your home by talking to an innkeeper.

## Finding an inn

Inns are marked on your minimap with a pale pin that shows a door. The pin's point stands on the door of the house the innkeeper is in, so walk to it and go inside. Zoom the minimap out to look for one further away.

An innkeeper who stands out in the open, with no house, is marked where they stand.

## Setting your home

1. Walk up to an innkeeper and talk to them.
2. Choose **Make this inn your home**.
3. Confirm with **Make it my home**.

You see "You have updated your home location". From then on your home item brings you back to the spot where you were standing when you set it.

You can change your home as often as you like by doing the same at another innkeeper.

:::note No home yet?
Until you set a home, your home item takes you to the world's starting point.
:::

## Using your home item

Use the item from your bags or your hotbar, like any [consumable](#/player-guide/consumables). The tooltip reads "Use: Returns you to your home inn."

A 10 second cast starts, shown on your cast bar. When it finishes, you arrive at your home.

The cast is broken by:

- Moving. You have to be standing still to start it, too.
- Taking any damage.
- Being stunned.
- Dying.
- Pressing **Escape**.

A broken cast costs you nothing. You can start again straight away.

## The cooldown

After you arrive, you have to wait 1 hour before you can use the item again. A clock is drawn over the item while you wait, with the time left written on it: `1h` at first, then the minutes, and seconds in the last minute.

This wait is separate from the 30 second cooldown your potions and food share. Neither one holds back the other.

The hour keeps counting while you are logged out.

## Good to know

- The home item is never used up.
- You cannot delete it, trade it or sell it.
- If you somehow have none, you are given a new one the next time you log in, as long as you have a free bag slot.
- If the innkeeper you chose is no longer there, the item takes you to the world's starting point until you set a new home.
