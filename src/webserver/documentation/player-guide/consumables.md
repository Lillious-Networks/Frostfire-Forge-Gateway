Consumables are items you use up, such as potions and food. Using one gives you health, stamina or both straight away.

Hover over an item to see what it does. A consumable has a green line that starts with "Use:", for example "Use: Restores 50 health."

## Using a consumable

There are two ways:

- **From your bags.** Double-click the item. On a phone or tablet, double-tap it.
- **From your hotbar.** Press the slot's number key, or select the slot.

One is taken from the stack each time. The health and stamina are added at once, and never go above your maximum.

## Putting one on your hotbar

Drag a consumable from your bags onto a hotbar slot.

| What you see on the slot | What it means |
|--------------------------|---------------|
| A number in the corner | How many you have left |
| A greyed out icon and 0 | You have run out. The slot stays, and works again when you get more. |
| A dark clock sweeping round, with a time on it | The cooldown is running, and this is how long is left |

To move the slot, hold **Shift** and drag it to another slot. To remove it, hold **Shift** and drag it off the hotbar.

## The cooldown

After you use a consumable you have to wait 30 seconds before you can use another. The wait is shared: using a health potion also holds back your food and every other consumable.

The clock is drawn over every consumable in your bags and on your hotbar, so you can see when they are ready. Logging out does not clear it.

The time left is written over the clock:

| It reads | When |
|----------|------|
| `2h` | More than an hour is left. Hours are rounded up, so 1 hour 15 minutes reads `2h`. |
| `59m` | Less than an hour is left. These are the whole minutes remaining. |
| `42s` | Less than a minute is left |

## When you cannot use one

| Message | Why |
|---------|-----|
| "You cannot use another item yet." | The 30 second cooldown is still running |
| "Your health is already full." | Everything the item restores is already at its maximum, so nothing is used up |
| "... cannot be used in combat." | Some items, usually food, only work out of combat. Their tooltip says "Cannot be used in combat." |
| "You must be level ... to use ..." | The item has a level requirement |
| "You cannot use items while dead." | Wait until you are alive again |
| "You cannot do that while stunned." | Wait for the stun to end |
| "You cannot use items while trading." | Close the trade first |

:::tip An item that restores two things
An item that gives both health and stamina can be used as long as one of the two is not full.
:::

## Where to get them

Consumables drop from creatures, come as quest rewards, and are sold by [vendors](#/player-guide/vendors).

Your home item is a consumable too, but it is never used up. See [Inns and Your Home](#/player-guide/inns).
