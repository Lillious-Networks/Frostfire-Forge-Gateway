The world is made of maps that are linked together. Two tools help you find your way: the full world map and the minimap.

## The world map

Press **M** to open the world map. It fills the screen and opens centred on you. The name of the map you are on is shown as its title.

The map marks:

- **You**, so you always know where you are.
- **Warps**, the places that lead to another map. Zoom in to read where each one leads.

| Action | Mouse and keyboard | Touch |
|--------|--------------------|-------|
| Zoom | Scroll the mouse wheel | Pinch with two fingers |
| Move the map | Drag it | Drag with one finger |
| Follow yourself | Select the **Centre on player** button | Select the same button |
| Close | Press **M** or **Esc**, or select the close button | Select the close button |

### Centre on player

The **Centre on player** button zooms in on your character and keeps the map centred on you as you move. While it is on, scrolling or pinching only changes how close the view is. Drag the map to let go, or select the button again to return to the view you had before.

:::note Caves and other sectioned maps
On maps that are divided into separate sections, such as cave systems, the world map shows only the section you are standing in.
:::

## The minimap

The minimap is always on screen and shows the area around you.

| On the minimap | Meaning |
|----------------|---------|
| Your marker with an arrow | Where you are and which way you face |
| Dots | Other players nearby |
| Skeleton icon | Your body, while you are a ghost. If it is outside the minimap, the icon sits on the rim in its direction. |
| Top label | The current time in the game world |
| Bottom label | The name of the map you are on |

Scroll the mouse wheel over the minimap to zoom it in or out. Your zoom level is remembered.

## Travelling between maps

Maps are connected by **warps**. Walk into a warp and you arrive at its destination. The world map shows where each warp leads.

You cannot use a warp:

- While you are in combat. Wait about 5 seconds after the last hit.
- While you are dead or a ghost.

Riding a mount gets you between warps faster. See [Mounts](#/player-guide/mounts).

## Day, night and weather

The world has a time of day, shown at the top of the minimap, and the light changes with it. Realms can also have weather, such as rain or snow.

## Finding your way after death

If you die and release your spirit, you appear at a graveyard as a ghost. A red marker at the edge of your screen points towards your body and shows how far away it is, and your body is marked on the minimap. See [death and respawn](#/player-guide/combat-and-spells/death-and-respawn).
