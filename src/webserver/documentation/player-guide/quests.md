Quests give you goals, experience, coins and items. You get them from characters in the world, and you hand them in to a character when you are done.

## Finding quests

Look for a marker floating over a character's head:

| Marker | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Gold exclamation mark | This character has a quest you can take |
| Grey question mark | You are working on a quest for this character |
| Gold question mark | You have finished a quest and can hand it in here |

Quests that need a higher level, or that follow a quest you have not finished yet, stay hidden until you qualify. New markers appear as you level up and complete quests.

## Talking to characters

Walk up to a character and press **E**. On a touch screen, tap the character.

- A character with a single quest for you opens that quest straight away.
- A character with several quests shows a list. Select the quest you want, or **Goodbye** to leave.
- A character without quests simply speaks in a bubble over their head. Talk again to hear their next line.

You have to stand close. If you are not, you see "You are too far from that NPC." If you walk away while the window is open, it closes.

## Accepting a quest

The quest window is a book with two pages. It shows the story, your objectives, and what you will earn.

- Select **Accept** to take the quest. It is added to your quest log.
- Select **Decline** to close the window. You can come back later.

Rewards are listed in two groups:

| Label | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| You will receive | Items you always get |
| Choose one | You pick one of these items when you hand the quest in |

Experience and coins are shown at the bottom of the page.

If **Accept** is greyed out, the window tells you why, for example "Your quest log is full." or "This daily quest is not available yet."

## Objectives

| Type | What you do |
|------|-------------|
| Kill | Defeat a number of a certain creature |
| Collect | Hold a number of a certain item. The count follows your inventory, so it goes down again if you lose the items. |
| Talk | Speak to a certain character |
| Explore | Visit a place |

Objectives update as you play. A finished objective gets a tick.

:::tip Kill credit in a party
When your party defeats a creature, members who are close by get credit for their own quests too.
:::

## The quest log

Press **L** to open the quest log. It lists your active quests, grouped by zone. A quest that is ready to hand in is marked **(Complete)**.

- Select a quest to read its description, objectives and rewards.
- Select **‹ Quests** to go back to the list.
- Select **Track** or **Untrack** to choose whether the quest is shown on your screen.
- Double-click a quest in the list to switch tracking quickly.

You can hold up to **25** active quests.

### The quest tracker

Tracked quests are listed on your screen with live counts for every objective, so you do not have to open the log. Up to **5** quests can be tracked at once. Select a quest's name in the tracker to jump to it in the quest log.

### Abandoning a quest

Open the quest in the log and select **Abandon**. The button changes to **Really abandon?** Select it again within a few seconds to confirm. An abandoned quest leaves your log, and you can take it again from its quest giver.

## Handing in a quest

1. Finish every objective. The quest is marked **(Complete)**.
2. Go to the character with the gold question mark and talk to them.
3. If the quest has a **Choose one** reward, select the item you want.
4. Select **Complete**.

You receive your experience, coins and items right away. If the character has a follow-up quest, it is offered to you immediately.

:::warning Make room first
If the rewards do not fit in your bags, the hand-in is refused with "You don't have enough bag space." Nothing is lost. Free some slots and talk to the character again.
:::

## Daily and repeatable quests

Most quests can be done once. Two kinds are different:

| Kind | How it works |
|------|--------------|
| Repeatable | Can be taken again after you hand it in |
| Daily | Can be done once per day. Dailies reset at 03:00 UTC, and are available again after the reset. |

:::note
03:00 UTC is the same moment for every player, so the local time of the reset depends on where you live.
:::
