This page lists everything you can press, click or tap. Keys only work while you are not typing in a text box, so press **Esc** or send your message first if a key seems to do nothing.

## Movement

Move with **W** (up), **A** (left), **S** (down) and **D** (right). Hold two keys together to move diagonally, for example **W** and **D** to go up and right.

- Opening the chat box stops your character.
- You cannot move while the menu is open.
- Riding a mount makes you faster. See [Mounts](#/player-guide/mounts).

:::tip Using a gamepad
If a gamepad is connected, its stick moves your character. Gamepad buttons are not mapped to actions, so you still need the keyboard and mouse for everything else.
:::

## Targeting

Most spells need a target. You can pick one in several ways:

- **Left-click** a player, creature or character to target it.
- Press **Tab** to target the nearest player or creature in front of you. Press it again to step to the next one.
- **Left-click** empty ground to clear your target.

Your target's name, level, health and mana appear in the target frame. A target that gets too far away from you is dropped automatically.

## Default keys

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| W, A, S, D | Move up, left, down, right |
| Tab | Target the nearest player or creature in front of you, press again to cycle |
| 1 to 9, 0 | Cast the spell in hotbar slot 1 to 10 |
| Q | Mount or dismount |
| E | Talk to a nearby character. Next to loot you can take: tap to pick up the nearest item, hold to pick up everything nearby |
| F | Open a nearby loot chest |
| B | Open or close the inventory |
| C | Open or close the character window (stats and equipment) |
| P | Open or close the spellbook |
| L | Open or close the quest log |
| M | Open or close the world map |
| O | Open or close the friends list |
| G | Open or close the guild window |
| K | Open or close the collectables window (your mounts) |
| Enter | Open the chat box, press again to send |
| Up and Down arrows | In the chat box: step through messages you sent earlier |
| Esc | Close the world map, cancel ground targeting, cancel a spell you are casting, or open the menu |
| F2 | Show or hide the performance and debug readout, including the FPS counter |
| Shift | Hold while pointing at an equipment item to compare it with what you are wearing |
| Ctrl | Hold and drag a window to move it |

:::note Changing the keys
The keys are fixed. There is no key binding screen at the moment.
:::

### What Esc does

**Esc** always does the most urgent thing first:

1. If the world map is open, it closes the map.
2. If you are choosing where to place a ground spell, it cancels that.
3. If you are casting, it cancels the cast.
4. Otherwise it opens or closes the menu.

## Mouse controls

| Action | Result |
|--------|--------|
| Left-click a player, creature or character | Target it |
| Left-click empty ground | Clear your target |
| Right-click a creature | Target it and start attacking |
| Right-click a defeated creature that has loot for you | Open its loot window |
| Right-click a player | Open the player menu |
| Right-click a party member's frame | Kick (leader), or leave the party on your own frame |
| Right-click a name in the guild window | Kick (Guild Master), or leave the guild on your own name |
| Right-click a name in the friends list | Remove that friend |
| Right-click one of your buffs | Cancel that buff |
| Left-click a hotbar slot | Cast that spell |
| Left-click a spell in the spellbook | Cast that spell |
| Drag a spell from the spellbook to the hotbar | Put the spell in that slot |
| Shift + drag a hotbar slot | Move the spell to another slot, or drop it outside the hotbar to remove it |
| Double-click an equipment item in the inventory | Equip it |
| Double-click an equipped item | Take it off |
| Drag an item out of the inventory onto the world | Destroy it, after a confirmation |
| Scroll over the minimap | Zoom the minimap |
| Ctrl + drag a window | Move the window. Its position is remembered. |

### The player menu

Right-clicking another player opens a small menu:

| Entry | What it does |
|-------|--------------|
| Inspect | Shows that player's stats and equipment |
| Send Message | Starts a whisper to that player in the chat box |
| Invite to Party | Sends a party invitation |
| Add Friend or Remove Friend | Sends a friend request, or ends the friendship |
| Invite to Guild | Sends a guild invitation |

## Touch controls

On phones and tablets the game adds on-screen controls. Hold your device sideways: the game is played in landscape.

| Action | Result |
|--------|--------|
| Drag the joystick | Move |
| Tap the mount button | Mount or dismount |
| Hold the mount button | Open the collectables window |
| Tap the round menu button | Open the wheel of windows |
| Tap a player or creature | Target it |
| Tap the creature you already target | Start attacking it |
| Tap a defeated creature that has loot for you | Open its loot window |
| Tap a character | Talk to them |
| Hold your finger on a player | Open the player menu |
| Tap a hotbar slot | Cast that spell |
| Drag a spell from the spellbook to the hotbar | Put the spell in that slot |
| Hold a hotbar slot, then drag | Move the spell, or drop it outside the hotbar to remove it |
| Double-tap an equipment item | Equip it, or take it off if it is equipped |
| Tap a party member, guild member or friend | Open their menu |
| Tap outside an open window | Close the window |

The wheel of windows holds Inventory, Spellbook, Quest Log, World Map, Character, Friends, Guild, Collectables and Settings.

On touch devices your own name, health, mana and level are shown in a frame of their own.

## Windows

Opening one of the side windows closes the others, so only one is in the way at a time.

| Window | Key | What it is for |
|--------|-----|----------------|
| Inventory | B | Your items, bags and coins. See [Inventory and Equipment](#/player-guide/inventory-and-equipment). |
| Character | C | Your stats and equipped items. |
| Spellbook | P | Every spell you know. See [Combat and Spells](#/player-guide/combat-and-spells). |
| Quest Log | L | Your active quests. See [Quests](#/player-guide/quests). |
| World Map | M | The whole map. See [Maps and the World](#/player-guide/world-map). |
| Friends | O | Your friends and who is online. See [Friends](#/player-guide/friends). |
| Guild | G | Your guild, or the form to create one. See [Guilds](#/player-guide/guilds). |
| Collectables | K | Your mounts. See [Mounts](#/player-guide/mounts). |
| Menu | Esc | Settings, Manage Profile and Logout. |

## Menu and settings

Press **Esc** to open the menu. It has three buttons:

- **Settings** opens the options below.
- **Manage Profile** opens your account page in a new tab. See [Account Security](#/player-guide/account-security).
- **Logout** leaves the game and returns you to the login screen.

| Setting | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| FPS | Limits how many frames per second the game draws, from 30 up to 240 and above |
| Music | Music volume |
| Effects | Sound effect volume |
| Muted | Turns all sound off |

Your settings are saved with your character.
