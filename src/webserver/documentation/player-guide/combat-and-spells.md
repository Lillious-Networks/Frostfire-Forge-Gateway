Combat is built around spells. You pick a target, press a hotbar key, and the spell does the rest. This page explains the rules so you know why a spell worked, or why it did not.

:::note Guests cannot fight
Casting spells and attacking creatures need a full account.
:::

## Targeting

- **Left-click** a player or creature to target it, or press **Tab** to target the nearest one in front of you. Press **Tab** again to cycle.
- **Right-click** a creature to target it and start attacking it automatically. On a touch screen, tap the creature, then tap it again.
- Click empty ground to drop your target.

The target frame shows your target's name, level, health and mana. A target that moves too far away is dropped.

## The spellbook and hotbar

Press **P** to open the spellbook. It holds every spell you know. Point at a spell to read what it does.

The hotbar at the bottom of the screen has 10 slots, used with the keys **1** to **9** and **0**.

| What you want | How to do it |
|---------------|--------------|
| Add a spell to the hotbar | Drag it from the spellbook onto a slot |
| Move a spell to another slot | Hold **Shift** and drag it. On touch, hold the slot and then drag. |
| Remove a spell from the hotbar | Hold **Shift** and drag it off the hotbar |
| Cast a spell | Press its number key, or click the slot |

Your hotbar layout is saved with your character.

## Casting a spell

1. Pick a target, if the spell needs one.
2. Press the spell's hotbar key.
3. If the spell has a cast time, a cast bar fills up. When it is full, the spell fires.

Before a cast starts, the game checks a few things. If one fails, the spell does not start:

| Check | What you see or notice |
|-------|------------------------|
| The spell is still on cooldown | Nothing happens. The slot shows the cooldown. |
| You do not have enough mana | The spell does not start |
| The target is too far away | "Target is out of range" |
| Something blocks the way to the target | "Target is not in line of sight" |
| You or your target is in a no-PvP area | "You are not in a PvP area" |
| You are stunned or locked out of casting | Nothing happens |

A few more rules:

- There is a short pause of half a second between spells, so you cannot fire two in the same instant.
- You turn to face your target when you cast at it.
- Casting takes you off your mount.

### Cast times and moving

Some spells can be cast while moving. Others need you to stand still.

- A stand-still spell does nothing if you press it while you are walking.
- If you move during the cast of a stand-still spell, the cast is interrupted. You get the cooldown back.
- Press **Esc** to cancel a cast yourself. You get the cooldown back and can cast again straight away.

### Cooldowns

After a spell is used, it goes on cooldown and its slot is dimmed until it is ready again. Cooldowns are shown both on the hotbar and in the spellbook.

### Friendly spells

Heals and other helpful spells only work on you and your party members. If you cast one while an enemy or a stranger is targeted, it lands on you instead of failing.

## Kinds of spells

| Kind | How it works |
|------|--------------|
| Targeted | Hits the player or creature you have targeted |
| Area around the target | Also hits everything close to your target |
| Area around you | Hits everything close to you. No target needed. |
| Ground targeted | You choose a spot on the ground. See below. |
| Charge and teleport | Moves you to your target at once. These fail if the path is blocked. |

Area damage never hurts you or your party members. Area healing only reaches you and your party members.

### Ground targeted spells

1. Press the spell's key. Your cursor turns into a crosshair.
2. Click the spot where you want the spell.
3. Press **Esc** instead if you change your mind.

The spot has to be within the spell's range. Some ground spells leave a zone behind that keeps affecting anyone standing in it until it fades, so step out of enemy zones.

## Effects that last

Spells can leave effects on you or your target. Your active effects appear as icons with timers. Point at an icon to read what it does.

| Effect | What it does |
|--------|--------------|
| Damage over time | Deals damage again and again for a while. Some stack. |
| Heal over time | Restores health again and again for a while |
| Barrier | Absorbs damage before your health is touched. Shown over your health bar. |
| Stun | You cannot move or cast until it ends |
| Slow | You move more slowly |
| Vanish | You are invisible to everyone except your party. Taking damage or casting a harmful spell ends it. |
| Interrupt | Stops a cast in progress and blocks all casting for a short time |

:::tip
Right-click one of your own helpful effects to cancel it early. Harmful effects cannot be cancelled this way.
:::

## Health and mana

Your health, mana and experience bars are part of the hotbar. On touch devices, health and mana are shown in your own frame instead.

- **Health** is how much damage you can take. At zero you die.
- **Mana** is spent to cast spells.
- **Experience** fills as you defeat creatures and complete quests. When it is full you gain a level.

Both health and mana come back on their own:

| Resource | Regeneration |
|----------|--------------|
| Mana | About 5% of your maximum every second, even in a fight |
| Health | About 1% of your maximum every second, but only out of combat |

You count as being in combat for 5 seconds after the last hit you dealt or took. While in combat you also cannot mount, cannot change equipment and cannot travel through warps.

Press **C** to see your stats: Level, Health, Mana, Damage, Armor, Critical Chance, Critical Damage and Avoidance. Better equipment raises them. See [Inventory and Equipment](#/player-guide/inventory-and-equipment).

## Fighting creatures

- The first player to hit a creature claims it. A creature claimed by someone outside your party shows a grey name, and gives you nothing.
- Defeating a creature you claimed gives experience, and often loot and coins.
- Creatures well below your level give less experience, and eventually none. Tougher kinds of creatures give more.
- In a party, experience is shared between the members who are close by. See [Parties](#/player-guide/parties).

Looting is explained on the [Inventory and Equipment](#/player-guide/inventory-and-equipment/loot-from-creatures) page.

## Death and respawn

When your health reaches zero you die. Your body stays where it fell, and a window appears with one button.

### 1. Release your spirit

Select **Release Spirit**. You become a ghost and are taken to the nearest graveyard. While you wait to release, you cannot move, fight or use say chat, but party, guild and whisper chat still work.

### 2. Being a ghost

As a ghost the world looks grey. You can walk, and you move faster than normal, but you cannot cast, attack, mount, loot or use warps. Nobody can hurt you.

### 3. Come back to life

You have two ways back:

| Way back | How | Cost |
|----------|-----|------|
| Return to your body | Walk back to where you died. A marker points the way, and your body is shown on the minimap. When you are close, select **Revive**. | None. You return with half your health and mana. |
| Resurrect at the graveyard | A few seconds after you arrive, the graveyard offers **Resurrect**. | You return with half your health and mana, and with Resurrection Sickness for 15 minutes. |

**Resurrection Sickness** lowers your maximum health by 20% and all your other stats by 10% until it wears off. It stays with you if you log out and back in.

:::tip
If the graveyard offer is in your way, select **Cancel**. It comes back when you leave the graveyard and return.
:::

## PvP and no-PvP areas

You can attack other players, except in no-PvP areas.

- If you or your target stands in a no-PvP area, harmful spells do not work. You see "You are not in a PvP area".
- You cannot place a harmful ground spell inside a no-PvP area. You see "You cannot cast that into a no-PvP area".
- Area spells never hurt players who are standing in a no-PvP area.

Some players can never be attacked:

| Who | Message |
|-----|---------|
| Members of your own party | "You cannot attack your party members" |
| Guests | "You cannot attack guests." |
| Ghosts | "You cannot attack ghosts." |

:::note
Remember the combat rule above: after trading blows with someone you stay in combat for 5 seconds, so you cannot instantly mount up or slip through a warp to escape.
:::
