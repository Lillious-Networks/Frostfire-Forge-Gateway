Your friends list keeps track of the players you like to play with, and shows you who is online right now.

Press **O** to open the friends list.

:::note
Friends need a full account. Guests cannot send or receive friend requests.
:::

## Adding a friend

You add friends by meeting them in the world.

1. Find the player in the game.
2. **Right-click** them and choose **Add Friend**. On a touch screen, hold your finger on the player to open the menu.
3. The player sees an **Invitation** window saying that you want to add them as a friend.
4. When they select **Accept**, you both see "You are now friends with" followed by the other player's name.

Friendship always goes both ways. When a request is accepted, each of you appears in the other's list.

:::tip
A friend request needs an answer, so both of you have to be online and near each other when you send it.
:::

## Seeing who is online

Open the friends list with **O**. Every friend is listed by name, with a marker that shows whether they are online or offline. The markers update as your friends log in and out.

If your list is long, type part of a name into **Search friends...** to filter it.

## Talking to a friend

Use a whisper to send a private message to a friend who is online:

```text title="Whisper a friend"
/w Aria Want to team up?
```

See [Chat and Commands](#/player-guide/chat-and-commands) for more about whispers. To play together, invite your friend to a party. See [Parties](#/player-guide/parties).

## Removing a friend

There are two ways:

| Where | How |
|-------|-----|
| Friends list | **Right-click** the friend's name and choose **Remove Friend**. On a touch screen, tap the name. |
| In the world | **Right-click** the player and choose **Remove Friend** |

Removing a friend also removes you from their list. You can add each other again later with a new friend request.
