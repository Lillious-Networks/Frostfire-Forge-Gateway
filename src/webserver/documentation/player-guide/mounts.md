A mount carries you across the world faster than you can walk. While mounted you move about **35% faster**.

## Your mount collection

Your mounts are kept in the collectables window. Press **K** to open it. Every mount you own is shown with its icon.

Mounts are added to your collection by the realm you play on. How you earn them depends on that realm, so ask around in chat if you are not sure.

## Riding a mount

1. Press **K** to open the collectables window.
2. Select the mount you want. You mount up right away.

After that you do not need the window:

| Action | How |
|--------|-----|
| Mount or dismount | Press **Q** |
| Switch to another mount | Dismount first, then select the other mount in the collectables window |

**Q** uses the mount you last selected from the collectables window.

:::tip Pick a mount first
After you log in, choose your mount once from the collectables window. If you press **Q** before that, you may see "You do not have the specified mount."
:::

### On a touch screen

- Tap the mount button to mount or dismount.
- Hold the mount button to open the collectables window and pick a mount.

## When you cannot mount

| Situation | What happens |
|-----------|--------------|
| You are casting a spell | "Cannot mount while casting." |
| You are in combat | "Cannot mount while in PvP." Wait about 5 seconds after the last hit. |
| You are dead or a ghost | Nothing happens |
| You do not own the mount | "You do not have the specified mount." |

You can always dismount, even in these situations.

## What takes you off your mount

- Pressing **Q** again, or selecting the mount again.
- Casting a spell. You are dismounted the moment the cast begins.
- Dying.

:::note
Being in combat does not throw you off a mount you are already riding. It only stops you from mounting up again once you are off.
:::
