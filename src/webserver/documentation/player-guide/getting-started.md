Welcome! This page takes you from nothing to standing in the world. It only takes a couple of minutes.

## What you need

- A computer, tablet or phone with a keyboard and mouse, or a touch screen.
- An up to date Chromium based browser such as Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave or Opera.
- An email address you can read, so you can receive verification codes.

:::note Why a Chromium based browser?
The game uses a modern browser connection feature that has no fallback. Chrome and Edge are the safe choices. If the game never finishes loading in another browser, switch to one of these.
:::

## Create an account

1. Open the game's home page. This is the login screen.
2. Select **Create new account**.
3. Fill in **Email Address**, **Username**, **Password** and **Confirm Password**.
4. Select **Create Account**.

Your details have to follow these rules:

| Field | Rule |
|-------|------|
| Username | 3 to 15 characters. Letters, numbers and underscores only. |
| Password | 8 to 20 characters, with at least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one number and one special character. |
| Email | A valid address that is not already used by another account. |

## Verify your email

After you select **Create Account**, the game sends a verification code to your email address and the form changes to a single **Verification Code** field.

1. Open the email and copy the code.
2. Type it into **Verification Code**.
3. Select **Verify**.

You will see a message that your email is verified, and you are sent back to the login screen.

:::tip Closed the page too early?
No problem. Log in with your username and password. The game notices that your email is not verified yet, sends a new code and asks for it on the login screen.
:::

## Log in

1. Enter your **Username** and **Password** on the home page.
2. Select **Login**.
3. If the game asks for a **Code**, check your email, type the code and select **Verify**.

New accounts start with email codes switched on, so expect a code by email each time you log in. You can change this, or add stronger options, on the profile page. See [Account Security](#/player-guide/account-security).

:::note One session at a time
If you log in somewhere else while your character is in the game, the older session is disconnected with the message "You have been logged in from another location."
:::

## Play as a guest

If the game has guest play switched on, select **Continue as guest** on the home page. You get a temporary character straight away, with no email or password.

Guests can walk around and look at the world, but a lot is locked. As a guest you cannot:

- Chat or use chat commands
- Cast spells or attack creatures
- Loot creatures
- Join a party or guild, or add friends

Other players cannot attack guests either. When you try a locked feature you will see "Please create an account to use that feature."

:::note Guest characters are temporary
Do not expect a guest character to be kept. Create an account if you want to keep your progress.
:::

## Pick a realm

After logging in you arrive at **Select a Realm**. A realm is a game world you can play on.

Each realm in the list shows:

| Detail | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Name | The realm's name. |
| Players | How many players are connected, and the maximum. |
| Status | **Online**, **Full** or **Offline**. Offline realms cannot be selected. |
| Latency | How quickly the realm answers you, in milliseconds. Lower is better. |
| whitelist | Shown on realms that only let approved accounts in. |

Select a realm to read its description, then select **Enter Realm**. The list refreshes by itself every few seconds.

:::tip
If you have a choice, pick the realm with the lowest latency. The game will feel more responsive.
:::

## Your first minutes in the game

When the loading bar finishes you are in the world. A few things to try first:

- Move with **W**, **A**, **S** and **D**.
- Press **Enter** to chat.
- Press **E** next to a character with a marker over their head to talk to them.
- Press **Esc** to open the menu, where you find **Settings**, **Manage Profile** and **Logout**.

The full list of keys is on the [Controls](#/player-guide/controls) page.

## Playing on a phone or tablet

The game runs in the browser on touch devices too.

- Hold your device sideways. The game is played in landscape, and asks you to rotate if you hold it upright.
- A joystick appears for movement, with a mount button and a round menu button for your windows.
- You tap things instead of clicking them.

See [touch controls](#/player-guide/controls/touch-controls) for the details.
